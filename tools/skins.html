VippeDash skin gallery

Every skin of every character (Art.CHARS / Art.SKINS in ../js/art.js), drawn the way the game draws them, for checking a new or changed skin with one screenshot. Parameters: char=<id> show only that character · skin=<id>[,<id>...] show only those skins · t=<seconds> freeze time (overrides performance.now to always return t*1000) so the page draws once and is pixel-identical between runs, for before/after diffing · scale=<n> multiply all sizes (default 1) · bg=light use a light sky-blue tile instead of mid-grey, to check contrast there too. Without t the page redraws every frame so shimmering patterns and hats (rainbow, galaxy, gold, robot antenna, fire, propeller, ...) can be watched live.

Sizes come from the game: the cube/ball are drawn at BS = 48 px in play (js/render.js), the ship at BS * 1.15, shop cards at 56 px and the shop tab icon at 28 px (drawShop() in js/game.js), and the menu hero at 96 px (drawHero()). The expressions the game actually uses are grin (default), o (mid-jump, v.oT > 0), happy (won) and tongue (crashed) — see R.drawPlayer in js/render.js and the eyes()/mouth() helpers in js/art.js. In play the cube looks with look=1; the shop and menu hero use look=0.
